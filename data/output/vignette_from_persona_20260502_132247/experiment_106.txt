channels back into that certainty.

than re-immersion. Incomprehensible background chatter — overheard conversations in open-plan

punishing gym sessions after work, a physical exhaustion she pursued with the focused determination

also meant the belief was never challenged. The avoidance of medical cues, which had begun as a

running, but from the management of it all. What she most feared was that this was simply who she
was now. What she most hoped, though she offered it quietly and without much conviction, was that it
