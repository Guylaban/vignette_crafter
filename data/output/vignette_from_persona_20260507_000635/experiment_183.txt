lingering effects of a life-threatening accident that occurred six months ago. Lena was involved in

shattered. The night of the accident, Lena remembers every detail vividly. She had been driving

to collide head-on. Her car spun out of control several times before coming to rest against a tree
on the side of the road. In the chaos that followed, she was forced by paramedics to get out of the
vehicle despite feeling dizzy and nauseous from her injuries. This loss of autonomy was a
significant trigger for her, as it felt like another imposition of helplessness in an already
traumatic situation. Since then, Lena has developed several avoidance behaviors. She avoids driving
altogether, opting instead to walk or use public transportation whenever possible. Even as a
passenger, she experiences intense anxiety and often breaks out into cold sweats. The thought of
being in a vehicle again is overwhelming; the memories of that fateful night make her feel
physically unwell, and she dreads reliving those sensations. Lena’s work has also been affected by
her symptoms. She now avoids activities that require physical exertion or stress on her body, such
as lifting heavy objects or walking long distances to deliver groceries. This avoidance has led to
tension with some of the individuals in her community who rely on her assistance. They have noticed
her reluctance to engage in these tasks and have begun to question why she is no longer as present
or available for help. In addition to these avoidances, Lena’s daily life is marked by a constant
sense of threat. She scans every room or building for exits the moment she enters, feeling that it's

more isolated. Despite assurances from friends and family that she is safe now, Lena finds it

unsafe. The cognitive distortions Lena experiences are deeply intertwined with her perceptions of

she takes. Lena’s body also feels unreliable to her, another source of anxiety and discomfort. She
often experiences unexplained physical symptoms such as dizziness or nausea, which only reinforce

feels safe and capable once more.
