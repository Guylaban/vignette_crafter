triggers that flood her with intense anxiety. The trauma began when Laila’s daughter was diagnosed

child's life hanging by a thread. These sounds and environments are now potent triggers that elicit
intense distress. Laila has come to associate these cues with the overwhelming sense of helplessness
she felt during her daughter’s illness. Since then, Laila has developed maladaptive strategies to
cope with the trauma. She tries hard not to think about what happened, often suppressing any
intrusive thoughts or memories. However, this effort to avoid thinking about the event only leads to
increased anxiety and a nagging sense of incompleteness. She is constantly engaging in meaning-
seeking behaviors, trying to understand why this tragedy befell her family. Despite these attempts

colleagues who have noticed her growing disengagement. Her daily life is also marked by significant
distress. Laila struggles to relax, even when she knows she is in a safe place. The constant state

beeping of machines—each one triggering a flood of anxiety. Sleep problems have also become a
significant issue. Laila has trouble falling and staying asleep due to her racing thoughts and vivid

any possibility of recovery. What finally brought Laila to seek help was the realization that her
