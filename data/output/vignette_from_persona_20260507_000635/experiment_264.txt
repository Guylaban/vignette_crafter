flames. Smoke filled the air, creating a thick haze that made it nearly impossible to see or

triggers for him because they reminded him so vividly of that terrifying day. Even now, he could
feel his heart racing whenever an ambulance siren wailed outside the hospital where he worked. The
burnt smell from a kitchen fire in a nearby building sent waves of grief through him as he was
transported back to the scene of the earthquake. In the weeks and months following the disaster,
Carlos began developing avoidance behaviors that impacted both his personal and professional life.
