avoided going out, avoided people, avoided the situations that might trigger the cascade of
sensations and shame. But each time he stayed home instead of showing up, each time he chose

evidence, in his mind, of his fundamental inadequacy. Rajesh had finally made an appointment with a
therapist last month, after Priya found him at three in the morning sitting in the dark of their
bedroom, unable to explain why he could not come to bed beside her. What frightened him most was not
the memories themselves, but the possibility that she would eventually see him as he saw himself—as
someone irreparably damaged, someone who had lost the person he used to be.
