hospital for follow-up appointments with dread building days in advance. Even the sight of an
ambulance outside a building, or the thought of a physical examination, made her tense and want to
back away. She began organizing her routes through the city to avoid passing medical centres, and if

in that helpless moment of waiting, when she could not tell from the voices around her whether her
