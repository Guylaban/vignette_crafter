else, not to him, and that large parts of it were missing. The parts that remained were so extreme
that he still found it hard to believe they had actually happened to him.

nothing had ever been done about it. He also carried a painful belief that it happened because of
the way he acted, as if one wrong choice in that chaotic moment had sealed the outcome. He did not

of it less as anger than as a stale, continuing outrage, the sense that the world had simply moved
on while he remained stuck with the consequences.
