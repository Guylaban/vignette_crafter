clear story. Sometimes he could recall what happened, but it felt completely numb, as if it had
happened to a stranger. At other times, especially when he allowed himself to think about those he
had lost, the memory came with overwhelming grief that left him standing in the kitchen for a long
time, unable to resume chopping or plating. He said he had never forgiven himself for not doing more
to stop what happened, even though he also knew, rationally, that he had been one person in a

for him; it surfaced most sharply on anniversaries, when he would picture people who had not made it

a way others could not see.
