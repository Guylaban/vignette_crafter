she understood, with a sinking certainty, that the situation was bigger than anyone there could
manage.

before rain, watching the clouds and listening for changes in the wind with a vigilance that made it
hard to focus on anything else. Nighttime was another point of difficulty; waking in the dark could
leave her abruptly on edge, her body reacting before she had fully oriented herself. She described
lying in bed after being roused by ordinary noises in the apartment building or by sounds from the

weather, and fearing most that she might always live as though she were waiting for the next
