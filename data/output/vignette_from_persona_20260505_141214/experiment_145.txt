with people she trusted she steered away from anything that might lead toward the war years. She
also tried hard to stay away from situations that made her feel strong emotion. She had come to
believe that emotional intensity made her vulnerable, so she preferred to keep herself busy,

sometimes helped her function well, but it also meant she passed up lunches, avoided social

overtaken by it.

check whether someone might be following her, even when she knew she was in an ordinary place such
