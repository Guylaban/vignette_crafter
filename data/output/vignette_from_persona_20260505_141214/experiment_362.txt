and what she had failed to do with relentless precision, searching for the exact point where things
went wrong. Since then, she had not been able to hold on to the version of herself she had once
taken for granted. In her mind, the teacher who had been careful, competent, and dependable was

capable of the things that had once defined her. The loss felt personal and total, not limited to
work but touching the core of who she believed she was.

now left her tense and off balance, as though the warmth was not meant for her or could not safely

attention.

exposed, as if others were seeing qualities she no longer trusted in herself. She began to shrink
