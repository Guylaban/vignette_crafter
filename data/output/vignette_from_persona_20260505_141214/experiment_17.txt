unsettled and silent.

ordinary sirens can jolt him; he stiffens when an ambulance passes and listens until the sound

thing out of his mind, but the effort often leaves him drained and more alone with it afterward.

stay alert to every shift in the environment. He has described feeling that he has to be on guard
all the time, and this stance shows up in how carefully he scans spaces before settling in. At other
times he has taken more risks than he would admit to others, almost as if testing whether danger
will find him anyway. These choices have made his days smaller and more restricted, and they have
also made social outings less spontaneous, since he needs time to assess any place before he can
