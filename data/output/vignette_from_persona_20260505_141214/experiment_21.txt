sense that those parts of her body had become impossible to see without feeling his chest tighten.

telling himself he could manage if he did not make a fuss.

happened. When someone asked direct questions about that relationship, or when a story came up
involving abuse, Mikaere would feel his jaw lock and his mind go blank in a way that made him want
to change the subject immediately. He avoided talking about it with anyone, including family, and he
was especially uneasy around people connected to the relationship, even indirectly, because their
presence seemed to pull him back into the same helplessness. He found himself arranging his days so

once the moment passed.
