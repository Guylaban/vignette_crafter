seemed to belong to the same world as the combat scene she still could not fully assemble. When she
woke from nightmares, the residue of them clung to her for hours. She would sit up in bed with her
heart racing, needing a few moments to understand where she was, then lie rigidly awake, listening
for threats she could not name. Even when she knew she was at home, part of her remained convinced
that danger could appear without warning.
