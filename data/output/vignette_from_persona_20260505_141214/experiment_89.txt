into that crash. Seeing cars hit by accident in videos or in the distance on the road would stop him
cold, because the image of smashed vehicles carried the same violent force as the day of the

tense before he was fully aware of it, his attention snapping toward the road as if bracing for
impact. Even the smell of alcohol became difficult to tolerate. If he caught it on someone’s breath

those cues were enough to bring the crash back with alarming force.

again in real time. At those moments, the memory did not remain partial or hazy. It could come back
across all his senses at once, feeling completely real, with the same sense of noise, impact, and

the crash was over, yet his experience of it could return with such vividness that for a few moments
he lost his footing in the present.
