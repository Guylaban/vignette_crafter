it. The smell of alcohol remained particularly potent for her. She could encounter it in passing, on

possibility that something bad might happen again.

emotional closeness and intimacy, keeping relationships carefully bounded and steering conversations

letting down her guard would leave her exposed. At home and at work she became increasingly focused

efficient and composed, yet she was less likely to stay after work for social time or accept
