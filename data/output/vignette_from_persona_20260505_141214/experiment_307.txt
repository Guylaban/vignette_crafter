way to know who would hurt him and when.

a film that played over and over, always with the same frightening scenes and same unbearable sense

happen. At work, this showed up in small but important ways. When his housekeeping shifts required

sound as something tied to those early years. He would tense, go still, and feel a rush of physical

around the corner, and to learn whether there was a way to be alone in a room, in the dark, or with
