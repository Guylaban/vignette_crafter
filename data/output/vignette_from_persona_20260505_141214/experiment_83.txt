it left her shaky and unable to collect herself. These episodes could be set off by something as

time to think.

happened. Over time, she began to feel that her life was no longer worth living in the way it had
been before, not because she wanted to die, but because she could not see how to inhabit the same

level that she had not caused it.

people when it mattered most.
