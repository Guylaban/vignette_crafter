to abandon his bike and flee on foot, leaving behind a visceral impression of inescapable pursuit

invaded. A discarded coffee cup on a sidewalk or the sudden scuff of tires against asphalt would
instantly drag the full weight of the stalking episode back into his mind. He knew exactly what had
happened, yet whenever he tried to explain it to his wife, the words dissolved into a choked
silence. The emotional surge would hit him so violently that he had to stop whatever he was doing,
gripping the kitchen counter until his breath evened out. Because these intrusions arrived without
warning, the moment familiar locations and the mere suggestion of being tracked surfaced, they

of being tracked.
