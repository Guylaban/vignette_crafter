in the pediatric ICU where Mila had been intubated and Leilani had not slept. Mila had recovered
fully, with no lasting neurological damage, but something in Leilani had not recovered at all.

conflict, even a mild one, was a sign that something catastrophic was about to happen. She started
avoiding conversations that might turn tense. She stopped calling her mother because her mother

could not survive that again.
