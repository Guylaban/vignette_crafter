not feel like a memory. It felt like it was happening right now, again, and he could do nothing to
stop it.

world was a dangerous place in a way he had never truly understood before. He had always known crime

afraid of what it would mean to let go of that vigilance—as if the only thing keeping him alive was
the scanning, the noticing, the never relaxing. He did not know what he would be without it.
