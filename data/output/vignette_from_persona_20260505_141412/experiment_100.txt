a police shooting of an unarmed man, or that the officer was killed by friendly fire. He only knew
that something inside him had snapped.

In the weeks that followed, Marcus tried to go back to his life. He was a commercial airline pilot,

rules. The first time he drove with a friend in the passenger seat—his copilot, Dave, who asked for

cabin, the same helplessness pressing down on him.

taking unpaid leave when he could. He avoided driving anywhere he did not have to. He started to
feel nauseous whenever he thought about getting behind the wheel, even alone.

like he was dying all over again. He did not know if that was possible, but he had finally stopped
pretending it would go away on its own.
