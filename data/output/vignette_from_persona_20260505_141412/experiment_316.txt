mouth. He never called the police. He never told a soul. He simply got up, cleaned the floor, and
went to bed.

the sound of her voice, the smell of the dinner she had brought home. Then, just as suddenly, they
vanished, leaving him disoriented and ashamed. He had come to believe that nothing good could happen
to him anymore. The world felt flat, drained of possibility. He moved through his days like an

know if he could be put back together, but he had come here, to this room, because he had nowhere
else to go.
