the flash of her arm raised again. Those moments still come to him as isolated pictures rather than

detached that he says it is as if it belongs to someone else, some older man he is watching from
outside.

fully registered the comment. Scenes of violence on television had a similar effect, and on the rare
occasions he caught sight of his former partner in the neighborhood, the rest of the day was gone.

himself in work, turn on the radio, switch tasks, do anything to distract himself. He also worked
hard not to feel too much of anything, because strong emotion seemed to open the door to the worst
of it. The trigger and the reaction had become almost fused: criticism, violence, or any cue of the
offender, and Tane was already reaching to shut down, look away, and mentally move elsewhere.

assault, vivid and disjointed. When he encountered his former partner unexpectedly, the memory did
