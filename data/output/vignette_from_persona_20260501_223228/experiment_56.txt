shaped how she read the present. Being alone with women could feel immediately dangerous to her,

signaled someone coming toward her—had a similar force. So did sudden silence. In the woods, the
abrupt hush after birdsong stopped could make her body brace before she had consciously understood
why.

not being exposed. At work she preferred tasks that kept her moving across open tracts rather than

an excuse to keep another person nearby. She also stopped most forms of exercise years ago. She said
exercise made her too aware of her body and breath, and she disliked anything that left her feeling

little reason she felt she had to trust anyone.

left underneath the habits.
