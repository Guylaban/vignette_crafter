made him a target, and that whatever future he had imagined for himself had ended there.

began avoiding anything that felt remotely stressful. At work, where he had once been known for

delegated high-stakes presentations and delayed difficult conversations if he could. He also went

seemed shrunken and joyless to him, which only confirmed his sense that nothing good was ahead and
that he was no longer the man he had been.

feeling briefly foolish but more afraid of what would happen if he stopped checking. When someone
touched him unexpectedly—a colleague brushing past him in a conference room, his wife placing a hand

intellectually that he was home and not imprisoned, yet he had come to believe danger could appear
without warning and that trusting apparent safety was exactly how people got hurt.
