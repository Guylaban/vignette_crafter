seeing a parent standing rigidly at a child’s bedside can abruptly pull him out of the present. In

again watching his daughter slip away. He has become constantly watchful, scanning rooms and
hallways before he even realizes he is doing it.

and keeps himself occupied almost continuously—taking extra charting home, staying late to review

push it away.
