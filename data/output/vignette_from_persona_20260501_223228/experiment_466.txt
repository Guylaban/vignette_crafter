she was back in danger. A packed briefing room or a congested terminal was enough to make her scan
for exits and think, with mounting certainty, that the world was simply dangerous and that safety

things happened before she had any chance to stop them.

used to be and strangely defenseless despite a career built on composure under pressure. She often
told herself that it could have been worse, that other people died and she did not, but this

helpless in the face of danger and doubted her ability to manage distress, she began structuring her

danger does not escalate, so each return to a cramped elevator, a packed operations room, or even a

a more private hope: to stop feeling that danger is everywhere and to find out whether she can still
trust herself when her body and the world both seem suddenly unsafe.
