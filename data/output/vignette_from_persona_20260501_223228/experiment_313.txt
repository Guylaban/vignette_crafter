police detail assigned to street work. He felt his body surge, lost track of the conversation around
him, and spent the rest of the shift detached and shaky, unable to remember parts of what had been
said to him. What frightened him most was not just the reaction itself, but the sense that his life
has become organized around anticipating danger and retreating from it. He says he wants “to be here
when things happen instead of somewhere else,” and he is afraid that if nothing changes, he will
remain trapped between frozen pictures of the past and a future he cannot bear to imagine.
