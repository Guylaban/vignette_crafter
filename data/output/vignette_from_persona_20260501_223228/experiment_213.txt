longer knew how the pieces fit together.

to believe that in the chaos she had acted against her deepest values, that whatever she did or

merely as reminders; they seemed to accuse her, pulling up the conviction that she was morally
stained, cut off from other people, and unsure whether her life held enough worth to continue as it
was. She described feeling that she belonged nowhere now—not with civilians who had never seen such

more guarded with people and more certain that sudden movement signaled danger. If someone reached

instantly on edge.

brought immediate relief, but over time it left her more alone and more convinced that she no longer
