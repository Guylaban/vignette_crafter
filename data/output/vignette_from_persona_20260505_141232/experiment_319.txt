been working as a relief worker in a war zone before returning home and eventually resuming civilian

the worst period of her deployment, the memory seems to stop short in her hands.

her account breaks apart. She knows something terrible happened in the middle of that shift, but

beginning before she mentally pushes it away, almost reflexively, before anything more complete can
come.

place her back at the edge of that day. A taste in her mouth could do it. During one session she
