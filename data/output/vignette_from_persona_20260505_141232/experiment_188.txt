still caught up in what she should have said, whether she should have insisted on stopping, whether
she should have done something different before the impact.

she will never forgive herself for not doing more to stop what happened. Even when other people told

everything, and failing to act in exactly the right way became proof to her of a serious personal
failing.

Since the collision, ordinary moments have acquired a charged and dangerous quality. Arguments are

answers messages or agrees to meet.

When thoughts of the crash crowd in too intensely, Linh sometimes hurts herself physically to stop

force her mind onto something else. She is ashamed of this and has hidden it from nearly everyone,

friends could no longer reach. At the same time, she still holds tightly to the fear that if she
lets down her guard, fails to anticipate danger, or trusts too easily, something just as devastating
