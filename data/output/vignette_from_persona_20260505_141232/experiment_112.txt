quickly or ask a coworker to take over, giving some practical excuse rather than saying what is
happening.

ordinary sick day.

has come to treatment worried that he is becoming more detached from people and from any place that
feels like home. At the same time, he hopes for some way to tell what happened without it seeming
unreal, and for a life in which a sore neck, a brief illness, or a weather image no longer makes the
disaster feel as if it is happening all over again.
