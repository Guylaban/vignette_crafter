most days are spent thinking about user flows, edge cases, and how one small decision on a screen

The event happens on a rainy weekday evening after she stays late to finish revisions for a product

shape of a motorbike coming through the intersection, the impossible thought — no, no, no — and then
the impact.

The rider is thrown to the pavement. She is out of the car before it fully stops rolling. She
remembers kneeling in the rain, hearing herself ask if he can hear her, hearing someone shout to
call an ambulance, seeing blood near his leg at an angle that tells her something is terribly wrong.

answering the same questions from police, from staff, from her husband when he arrives pale and
breathless. She learns the rider survives but has multiple fractures and a serious head injury.
Relief comes for a second and is immediately overtaken by something heavier: she did this. Someone
else’s life is now split into before and after because of one movement of her hands.

that these things happen, that she should be grateful no one died. She nods, but inside she rejects
every effort to soften it. At night she sees the rider’s body lifting from the bike just as she
