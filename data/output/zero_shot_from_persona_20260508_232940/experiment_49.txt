The rupture begins on a weekday afternoon in the city, in a commercial building where he has gone

and on. He drops instinctively, tastes plaster in his mouth, and feels a hot sting across his cheek
and neck. For several moments, maybe longer, he is certain he will die there.

sense of the idea that another person planned for strangers to be torn apart during an ordinary
