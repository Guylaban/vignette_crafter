at night. The fragmented nature of her memories only compounded her feelings of helplessness. When
she tries to recall what happened during the storm, the pieces do not fit together logically,

a cold sweat. At work, Mei's condition has become increasingly apparent. Her avoidance of loud

in both her daily routines and relationships. By addressing these fears and negative beliefs, she
