accident she caused that led to another person's serious injury. The traumatic event occurred during

physical and emotional distress. For instance, neck pain—a recurring symptom—reminds Martha not only
of the accident but also of her inability to prevent it. The sight or sound of cars crashing on TV

threat, making it difficult for her to concentrate and causing physical reactions such as sweating
and palpitations. Martha has developed a range of maladaptive strategies to cope with the

when triggered by sounds or images associated with the crash, Martha experiences vivid auditory
