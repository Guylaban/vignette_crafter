physically. Her husband noticed the changes but found it difficult to understand the depth of her
suffering. Aisha felt increasingly disconnected from those around her, believing that nothing would
ever be the same again. Over time, these behaviors created a vicious cycle. The more she dropped

avoidance. Her body remained on high alert, making it nearly impossible for her to find any sense of

belief that she was permanently damaged. Aisha finally sought help when she realized the toll these
behaviors were taking on her marriage and career. She feared losing everything that once gave her
purpose and identity. Her primary hope is to regain a sense of control over her life and find a way
