beginning of the crash all over again. She started avoiding anything that put her body under stress:
no gym, no running, no carrying heavy groceries. At work, where she managed database systems for a

like a trap door about to open.

course was to keep them at a distance. Her husband, Lee, grew frustrated. He could not understand
why she refused to go to dinner with their old college group, why she snapped at him when he asked

manage anything else?

being so afraid of her own body, her own husband, her own name.
