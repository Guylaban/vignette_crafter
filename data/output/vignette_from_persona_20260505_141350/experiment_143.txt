unguarded moment. She felt, in her bones, that she had to be on guard all the time.

throat. These pieces appeared fully formed, almost like a hallucination, and then vanished just as
quickly, leaving her disoriented. She would try to hold onto them, to make sense of the whole story,

went blank. She knew the fragments existed but could not string them into a coherent narrative. The

step behind her. But the vigilance wore her down. She was sleeping three hours a night, her body

asked too many questions, because the noise felt overwhelming and she needed to listen for danger.
She saw the hurt in their faces, and the shame was crushing.

sudden pieces will come back, and she will not be able to protect herself.
