hadn't. The officer eventually released him with a warning, but the damage was done.

In the weeks that followed, Mark could not shake the image of the gun barrel or the sensation of

feel like a trap. He would wake at three in the morning, disoriented, drenched in sweat, certain
that footsteps were approaching. Nighttime became something to dread rather than simply endure. At
first, he tried to push through, but his mind kept returning to that moment. He began to avoid
driving altogether, but his job as a graphic designer required him to commute to a downtown office.
