his wedding ring. Ali did not fight back. He remembered the pressure of the stranger’s forearm

belonged to someone else’s life. When his wife asked him what happened at the hospital, he tried to

floor, unable to finish.

In the months that followed, Ali found himself working longer and longer hours in the fields. He was
