wetland ecosystems across the Pacific Northwest. She carries herself with the quiet confidence of

everything, and without witnesses, the case went nowhere. Margaret returned to work two weeks later,
determined not to let it derail her. She was a scientist. She was rational. She would move forward.

mentored graduate students. But the trauma never truly left—it just learned to hide in her nervous
system, waiting.

Sleep is fragmented. She wakes most nights between two and four in the morning, her body drenched in

what the rest of her life will be.
