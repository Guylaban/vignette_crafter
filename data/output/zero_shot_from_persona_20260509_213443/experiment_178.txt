# Marcus

Marcus sits across from me with his shoulders curved inward, his posture suggesting he's trying to
take up as little space as possible. At twenty-seven, he has the careful movements of someone who

The relationship lasted three years. Marcus describes his ex-partner, David, as initially charming

office. A slap when he forgot to pick up groceries. Marcus rationalized each incident, told himself

the sound of his own voice pleading.

It took Marcus two weeks to leave. He filed a police report, moved in with his sister, and returned
to work because he didn't know what else to do.

Now, five months later, Marcus struggles with the simplest aspects of his job. He used to thrive in
the investigative process—the interviews, the uncovering of stories. Now, when he sits across from

looking for signs of aggression that might not be there. During a recent interview about workplace

hesitation, the way he's become more cautious in his reporting. Marcus worries he's losing the edge
