withdrawn, avoiding anything that could trigger memories of that fateful day. She's stopped driving

the accident scene. The physical pain she experiences in her neck, a lingering injury from the

overwhelmed by strong negative emotions: fear, horror, anger, guilt, and shame all swirl together in
