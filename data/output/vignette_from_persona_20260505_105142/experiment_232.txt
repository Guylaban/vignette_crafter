home. He started avoiding anything that reminded him of Alex - photos, mementos, even certain

living like this. The thought of facing his grief head-on was terrifying, but it was also the only
way Carlos could see to start rebuilding himself - and maybe, just maybe, find a way out of the
darkness that had consumed him.
