would go white, as if the language for it had been erased.

threat monitoring. He would watch the student’s hands, the way he leaned in, scanning for any

behind his eyes, and with them came the crushing feeling of being set apart from others, isolated by
something he could not explain. He had come to believe that other people lived in a world where
bodies were trustworthy; he no longer lived there.

decades earlier, because the ritual of it gave his hands something to do and his mind a narrow
