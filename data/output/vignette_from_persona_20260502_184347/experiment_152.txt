event into words either, not even now, decades later. When she tried to tell her late husband, the
words would not come, and she would only feel a surge of rage—hot and sudden—at the soldier, at the
war, at herself for failing.

control she had built. But she was also, quietly, hoping for something she could not name—a crack of
light in the numbness she had worn for so long.
