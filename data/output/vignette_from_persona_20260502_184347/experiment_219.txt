permanent fixture in his mind.

happened to someone else—a story so extreme it cannot possibly belong to his own life. Yet at other
moments, the memory crashes over him with terrifying immediacy. When he hears heavy breathing—a

This connection between triggers and memory has become the central loop of Rashid’s daily life. He

breathing—even the sound of his own exertion during exercise now makes him flinch.
