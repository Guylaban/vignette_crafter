have refused to stay buried.

raw terror of the explosion—the same visceral fear he felt at that moment, as if he is back in the
ravine. Worse than the fear is the guilt that surges with every memory. He has come to believe that

blue coveralls required by the warehouse, because they remind him of the uniform he wore that day.
