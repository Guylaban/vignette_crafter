the person who had survived that spin was not the same person who had entered it. He felt hollowed
out, as if his capacity for normal emotion had been stripped away, and he would say to his wife,

stranger who was always watching, always waiting.

not do it again. He is terrified that he will never fly without fear, that the person he was is gone
forever. But he is also, somewhere beneath the vigilance and the exhaustion, hoping that a part of
