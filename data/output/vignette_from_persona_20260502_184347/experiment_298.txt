broad shoulders and a certain confident gait—and would delegate their check-ins to her staff,
inventing reasons to stay in her office.

maladaptive strategies—especially the compulsive rumination about revenge, replaying scenarios of

would have moved on by now. This created a closed loop: her negative appraisals drove her to
overwork and avoid, which kept the memory fragmented and shame-laden, which reinforced her belief
