unfamiliar path that added twenty minutes to her commute. At work, where she designs residential
buildings, she found herself scanning the windows of her office for any sign of movement outside,
her heart racing whenever a car backfired or a door slammed in the hallway. She had come to believe
that the world was fundamentally dangerous, that violence could erupt at any moment, and that she
was powerless to prevent it. This belief colored everything: she stopped going out after dark,
declined invitations from colleagues to grab dinner, and began sleeping poorly.

wakes feeling as though she has not rested at all. During the day, she is superalert, constantly
scanning her surroundings for potential threats. She notices every person who walks too close to her

hears raised voices.
