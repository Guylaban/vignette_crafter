possible. Clara had not screamed. She had not even moved. She had stood there, frozen, while other
people rushed forward. The paramedics said later that Marcus would recover, but he would walk with a
limp for the rest of his life. Clara had come to believe that if she had been faster, if she had
shouted, if she had simply moved her body instead of standing like a mannequin, the whole thing
would not have happened. Somebody else would have known what to do. Somebody else would not have
gotten into that situation in the first place.

In the months that followed, Clara found that her body had become a stranger to her. She could not

that never came. This constant physical vigilance bled into everything. She had difficulty

her inability to let go became, in her own mind, further proof that she was fundamentally flawed.

herself for not doing more to stop it.
