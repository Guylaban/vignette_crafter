within a week the bodega was shuttered, the whole thing erased as if it had never happened.

In the months that followed, Margarita found that the memory of that night had a life of its own. It
would rise without warning—the smell of spilled coffee and copper, the weight of Carlos’s body

recollection but a full sensory reliving: she would feel the cold February air on her skin even if
