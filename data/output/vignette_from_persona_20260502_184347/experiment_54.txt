deployment, the word *platoon* drifting across the break room—and suddenly she was not in the break
room. She was back, and she could not trust that she would do the right thing, could not trust her

have done differently, why she had been chosen, what it meant. This rumination was not a search for

inter-agency meetings where military liaisons might be present. She took lunch at her desk, alone.
