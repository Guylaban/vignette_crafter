In the months following the attack, Fatima noticed a stark transformation in her emotional
landscape. She had once been a vibrant, joyful person, but now a shadow of that woman lingered,
replaced by a constant sense of misery. She often found herself reflecting on the happiness she had
lost, convinced that she would never reclaim it. The world felt dull and colorless, each day
blending into the next, devoid of the joy she once embraced. The disconnect from her former self was
palpable; she felt like a stranger in her own life. The more she ruminated on these thoughts, the
deeper the pit of despair grew within her, as if every negative appraisal solidified her identity as
a survivor marked by trauma rather than a person capable of thriving.

As time went on, Fatima's avoidance behaviors escalated. She began to withdraw from social
gatherings, fearing that her trauma would somehow spill over into her interactions with friends and
family. What had once been a source of joy and connection became a breeding ground for anxiety. She
felt as if she were living in a bubble, encased in her own fears and insecurities, while the world
continued to move forward around her. The distance grew, leaving her husband feeling helpless and
confused, unsure of how to penetrate the walls she had built around herself. She longed to be the
person she used to be, but the louder the helicopters grew in her mind, the more she felt her true
self slipping away.

Eventually, the weight of her emotions became too much to bear. Fatima reached a breaking point one
evening when she found herself unable to sleep, overwhelmed by the barrage of intrusive thoughts and
memories. It was then that she realized she could no longer navigate this alone. With a heart heavy
with fear and a glimmer of hope, she made the decision to seek help, yearning to reclaim her lost
happiness and identity. She knew the journey would be fraught with challenges, but the desire to
find herself again outweighed her apprehension. What frightened her most was the uncertainty of what
lay ahead and whether she could truly confront the painful memories that had taken root in her mind.
