To cope with these relentless triggers, Ethan had turned to alcohol. At first, it was just a drink
or two to dull the edges of his fear, but soon it became a nightly ritual. Each evening, he would
pour himself a glass of whiskey, hoping to escape the memories that clawed at him. He justified it
as a necessary escape, a way to silence the panic that gripped him when darkness fell. His nights
were often filled with blurred visions and a false sense of calm, but the mornings would bring
regret and renewed anxiety. He also found himself avoiding any situation where he might encounter
police uniforms or weaponry. This instinctive avoidance led him to skip out on social gatherings

that avoiding crowded places was the only way to keep the panic attacks at bay, reinforcing his
decision to stay home rather than face the anxiety of potential triggers. This instinctive avoidance
became a daily routine; he would deliberately choose routes to work that kept him far from police
stations, believing that if he could just stay away from those triggers, he might find some
semblance of peace. When he saw officers in the street or news reports depicting police action, he
would instinctively cover his face with his hands, as if shielding himself from the memories that
threatened to engulf him.

This avoidance, while offering temporary relief, only deepened his isolation. As he began to
withdraw from his social circles, the thought of facing his friends, and the potential reminders of
the trauma, made him drink more heavily, believing it to be a safer alternative to confronting his
fears. He began to withdraw from friends, no longer joining them for outings or social events, and
instead choosing the solitude of his apartment where he could safely numb his thoughts. The vibrant
city he once loved became a prison of his own making, filled with triggers and reminders of his
trauma. He felt trapped in a cycle of fear and avoidance, where each day blended into the next,
marked only by the rising levels of his alcohol consumption.

Ethan's decision to seek help came after a particularly harrowing night when he nearly succumbed to
a panic attack while driving home. As he passed a police car parked on the side of the road, his
heart raced, and he fought the urge to swerve away. That night, he realized he could no longer
navigate life in such a state. He was terrified of what his future could hold if he continued down
this path, feeling as though he was losing a grip on reality. With a mix of hope and trepidation,
Ethan made an appointment with a therapist, hoping to reclaim his life from the shadows of his past
and rediscover the joy that once defined his world.
