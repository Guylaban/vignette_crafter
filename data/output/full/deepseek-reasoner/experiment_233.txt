unbearable—he found it harder to feel safe at night than during the day, lying awake listening to
every creak of the house, convinced that darkness offered cover for anyone who might harm him. The

triggers seemed to jump out at him. He started leaving for work earlier to avoid rush hours and
began taking longer, quieter routes, even though they tired him out. His wife noticed he no longer

real—too real to ignore.
