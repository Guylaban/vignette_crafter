never arrived as a coherent story. Instead, Kiri was met with a wordless, overwhelming surge of
emotion that left her gasping on the bathroom floor, the trauma too vast to shape into sentences,
only a heavy, suffocating pressure and a white-hot rage. That unspoken terror quickly became its own
catalyst. The thought of remembering the ambush instantly sharpened her ear to sirens and loud
noises, turning them into immediate alarms. Her own pulse would begin to hammer against her
collarbone long before the sound faded, a somatic warning that made her shoulders lock and her mind
snap to irritability. This heightened sensitivity bled directly into how she navigated the hospital.

fear drove her to avoid exercise and decline invitations from her boyfriend, Marcus. She stopped
going to the hospital gym, convinced that physical exertion would spike her pulse too high, and she
turned down weekends or dinners because she was certain any shift toward joy would leave her
exposed. The triggers themselves directly fueled her threat state, making her irritable during
morning rounds and pulling her into a relentless scan for danger. The flashbacks would return
unbidden during charting, making her feel the heat of the desert and the weight of the wounded
beside her again, which in turn made her heart race and pulled her back to the memory of the blast.

moments and physical exertion, she denied herself the chance to recalibrate, which meant the
traumatic memory continued to intrude with greater frequency. The flashbacks and irritability fed

checking routine became a cage that tightened the loop. The strain rippled through her daily life.

bracing for a crash, and to finally rest in a room where she no longer had to check every corner.
