pounded, her muscles tightened, and she became certain that being in a crowd, hearing sirens, or

noticing that even a busy market or a line at the pharmacy made her scan for exits and for anyone
carrying something that could be used as a weapon. She could not relax in places others considered
safe, because the moment she felt crowded or heard a siren in the distance, her body seemed to
return to that street and she braced herself for harm.

the scene would arrive without warning and vanish just as quickly, leaving her disoriented and
tense. The more these fragments intruded, the more she tried to manage them by changing her
routines. She began staying up until very late because sleep felt like a vulnerable time, when the

night. In a grocery store she would feel a wave of physical reaction the moment someone brushed past
