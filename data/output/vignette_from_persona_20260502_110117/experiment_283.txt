time the paramedics left, Luis was gone. Afterward, funerals, cemeteries, and even the smallest
grief-related conversation seemed to carry her back to that night with terrible force; if a coworker

when sirens had filled the street and her brother had not come back.

In the weeks that followed, Marisol began going over and over the details of the event, replaying
