events in order. The memory felt unreal to her, almost impossible to believe had happened to her at
all, as if it belonged to someone else’s life. Still, certain moments pierced that distance: a

aftermath, with her heart racing and her body jolting before she could think.

whenever someone spoke about death or loss, because those conversations seemed to open the door to
the same helpless terror.
