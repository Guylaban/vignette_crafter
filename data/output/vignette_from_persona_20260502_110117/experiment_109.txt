danger everywhere, and the more quickly she reacted to unexpected movement nearby as though someone
were about to grab her.

that her life had narrowed around it. Mina hoped for relief from the constant scanning, and she was
most afraid that without help she would remain trapped in the same state of guard and shame, unable
to trust her own mind or feel safe anywhere.
