quite dropped. Priya knew on some level that she was safe in her marriage and in her apartment, yet

anything, she was afraid that if she kept avoiding the memory, it would continue to control her
reactions; and she was also afraid that if she let herself look at it fully, the feelings would
become too much to bear.
