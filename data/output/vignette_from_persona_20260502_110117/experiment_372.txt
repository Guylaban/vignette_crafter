Since then, he had come to believe that people could not be trusted, that the world was dangerous,
and that what happened to him had left him permanently humiliated.

even slightly suspicious. A man lingering too long near the front desk, someone pacing by the

laughter in the hallway could send a jolt through him. He had become convinced that staying watchful
was the only reason he remained safe, and that if he let his guard down, someone would take
advantage of him the way the mugger had.

racing, as if he had been grabbed from behind again. Even at home, where he had once felt settled,

he could relax enough to sit down.
