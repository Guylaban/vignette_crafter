inside her without warning, and she would look around, disoriented, certain she was back at the
roadside again.

the crash itself but her failure to stop it from becoming worse. She told herself, with a harshness

counted. Whenever a memory of the crash surfaced, it seemed to prove those thoughts all over again.

judge herself for her inaction, telling herself she would never forgive herself for not doing more.

feeling only strengthened her belief that her life was no longer worth living in the way it had
been. Certain sights made the shame flare again before she could get them under control: blood from
a paper cut, a bent fender on the street, cars that had been hit by accident at an intersection. In
those moments she felt the old panic and guilt surge together, as if the present scene had unlocked
the original one and confirmed her failures.

accidents, and the route she once knew so well began to feel like a place full of warning signs.
