benign kind everyone has—he jolts upright, certain this is the one. The last time he woke her to

was a UX designer. His job was to make things functional, frictionless. He hated friction. Now every
sensation in his body is friction.

change. Now he avoids those projects. His manager noticed, and he offered a story about needing
