Before, he painted the market. He loved the market. He would set up his easel near the fish stalls

hit a pile of mangoes or the glint of a knife as a fishmonger scaled a catch. People knew him there.
They would stop and look at his work, sometimes buy a small canvas to hang in their homes. He was
known as the painter who captured the life of the village.

eyes, there was dust everywhere and a ringing in his ears, and someone was screaming. He tried to
