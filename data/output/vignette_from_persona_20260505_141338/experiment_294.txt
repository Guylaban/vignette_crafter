her own will being utterly overridden—these sensations were burned into her very being.

often intruded with brutal force. A recent incident at work had been particularly jarring. Her new
manager, a young, assertive man, had unilaterally decided to implement a new, complex reporting

marches, the overwhelming feeling of her autonomy being stripped away. She sat frozen, unable to
