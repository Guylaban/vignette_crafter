right now, the dust in his throat, the metallic taste of fear, the sheer panic of being trapped.

time. When he returned to work at the rebuilt store, or even just walked into a new coffee shop, his
eyes would immediately scan for exits as soon as he entered any room or building. It wasn't a
conscious decision at first, but an automatic response, a primal urge to identify potential escape

slip out if danger arose. This constant scanning of his environment for signs of threat became
