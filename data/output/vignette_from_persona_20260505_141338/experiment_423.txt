stalker. He sped up, coming alarmingly close, his presence a suffocating weight just inches from her

pursuit, the feeling of being hunted, etched itself into her mind. Despite her reports to the

responsible had faced no consequences for the terror they inflicted.

In the months that followed, the world around Sarah began to transform into a landscape of potential
threats. The simple act of moving through her city became an an intricate exercise in vigilance. She
found herself constantly scanning her environment for signs of danger, her eyes darting from face to
face, car to car, bicycle to bicycle. The sight of a person on a bike, particularly if they were
approaching from behind or seemed to linger, would send a jolt of panic through her, her heart
pounding as if she were once again being pursued. What frightened her most was the feeling that she
often reacted as if she were in danger even when nothing was actually wrong, her body tightening,

leaping out of her skin.

positioning herself near an exit, a clear path to safety in her mind. This constant planning and
scanning made it difficult to truly relax or engage fully in conversations, leaving her feeling

constant internal alarm.
