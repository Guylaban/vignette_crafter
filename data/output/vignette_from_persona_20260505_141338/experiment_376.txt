constantly scanning her surroundings for any sign of danger, her gaze lingering on exits, on
shadows, on the hands of strangers. This hypervigilance extended particularly to people; she found
herself scrutinizing faces, movements, and expressions, certain that danger could lurk anywhere, in
anyone. What frightened her most was the belief that you could never truly know who would harm you.

of risk.
