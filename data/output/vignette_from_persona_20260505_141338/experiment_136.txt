stole not just her sense of safety but, she felt, the very future she and Robert had so carefully

surfaced, she would feel an intense, burning rage, a fury so consuming it left her breathless. Other
times, she would find herself pushing the memory away before she could fully recall it, a desperate
mental scramble to avoid the full picture. And then there were moments, unsettling in their
