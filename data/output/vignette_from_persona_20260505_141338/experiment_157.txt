shadowing his every move.

alcohol, a cold sweat would break out on his brow, his focus fractured by the overwhelming sensory
assault. He had come to believe that people were not what they seemed, that the facade of normalcy
could hide a capacity for immense cruelty, a conviction that made him distrust others profoundly.
