breathless.

meticulous, almost ritualistic habit of checking every door and window, not just once, but multiple

needing the tactile reassurance that they were truly secure. Yet, even this rarely brought peace. A
sudden creak of the old house, a distant siren, or the rustle of leaves outside his window would

threshold. He often reacted as if he were in imminent peril, his body tensing, his breath catching,
only to realize there was nothing actually wrong. The fear, horror, and a persistent, gnawing guilt
