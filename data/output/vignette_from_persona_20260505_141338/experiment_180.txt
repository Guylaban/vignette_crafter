environment, especially for vehicles. Any car that bore a passing resemblance to a police cruiser,

Liam began to rely heavily on alcohol. A few glasses of whiskey in the evening became a nightly
ritual, a way to quiet his racing thoughts and ensure he could fall asleep without the fear of
waking up disoriented in the dark. He found himself actively avoiding any situation where he might
encounter police or military personnel, taking longer routes to work to steer clear of the precinct,

regain some semblance of the life he had before, to feel safe in his own skin, and to believe that
the darkness of night could once again be just darkness, not a harbinger of terror.
