to happen. She experiences this frequently in her work, visiting clients in quiet homes or during a
lull in conversation in the office, her eyes darting around the room, anticipating a threat.

worst, her mind racing with catastrophic scenarios. This pervasive sense of unease means that even

utterly unable to relax; her body remains tense, her mind vigilant.

work, but now I am always miserable.* Her deep-seated faith, once the bedrock of her life and her
profession, has been profoundly shaken. She feels she has lost her faith not just in people, but in
life itself, and in the higher power she once served so devotedly. A profound shame has settled over
her, a deep sense of disgust and betrayal regarding her own body. *It feels tainted, no longer

measure of control over her body and her life, a desperate response to the profound loss of autonomy
she experienced.

These habits have taken a heavy toll over the past months. Her marriage, once a source of unwavering

Isabella finally sought help after a particularly difficult week where her inability to relax

once was, to find a path out of the misery and the profound sense of shame that had consumed her
