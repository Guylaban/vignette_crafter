cold, metallic smell of the hospital that seemed to permeate everything. He remembered the feeling

was dying, his very being reduced to a fragile, failing machine.

deliberately recall the details of his ICU stay, the specifics often eluded him, a jumble of blurred
images and disjointed sensations. Yet, a sudden whiff of alcohol, perhaps from the hand sanitizer a
teacher used in the hallway, or the antiseptic spray from the school nurse’s office, could instantly
transport him back. In those moments, a wave of intense fear would wash over him, his heart
