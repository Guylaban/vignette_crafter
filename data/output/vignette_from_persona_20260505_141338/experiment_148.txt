been completely shattered. He carried a heavy burden of guilt, a deep conviction that he had failed

loving wife.

notice. He found it increasingly difficult to concentrate on the complex financial documents before

particularly unsafe at night.

harder to bear.

dread and isolation, unable to ever truly feel safe or connected again.
