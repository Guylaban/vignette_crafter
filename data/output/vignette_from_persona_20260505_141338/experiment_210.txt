a profound sense of injustice and fear.

fields, once a comfort, now felt like a prelude to danger. She began to interpret ordinary

someone lingering by the edge of the property, she would immediately feel a surge of panic, her
heart pounding. *People are not what they seem*, she had come to believe, the memory of those
shadowy figures solidifying into a profound distrust of others. She found herself scanning faces,

a baby crying, whether from a neighbour's home or a passing car, would send a jolt of terror through

room, her stomach churning with dread.

some semblance of peace, to feel safe in her own skin again.
