his haste, and perhaps a momentary misstep of his own, he inadvertently bumped her. The customer

sound had been sickening. She sustained a severe concussion and a lengthy hospital stay, an outcome
George was certain was solely his fault.

Since that day, George’s life had fundamentally shifted. Memories of the incident were a constant

leaving him breathless. When he allowed himself to recall the event more fully, it brought back the

been irrevocably broken. His daily struggles only reinforced these beliefs; his reactions since the
event, he felt, showed that he was a lousy coper, unable to handle the simplest stresses without
feeling overwhelmed.
