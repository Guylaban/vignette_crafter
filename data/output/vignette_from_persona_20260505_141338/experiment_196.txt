the crushing weight, the desperate gasp for air, as if the frightening event were happening all over
again in that very instant.

the abuse must have been his fault, a consequence of some inherent flaw in his character. He was
certain that he was inadequate, not just as a partner, but as a person, and a profound sense of not
belonging anywhere began to isolate him from the world he once knew. He started to pull away from
his friends and even from his current partner, Maya, feeling increasingly disconnected and
misunderstood.

increasingly hostile. Every time he entered a new building or even a familiar café, his eyes would

tighten in his stomach; he was certain something terrible was happening, a direct echo of the
sudden, unpredictable violence he had experienced. At night, lying awake in his apartment, he would
listen intently for any sound outside his door, convinced that someone might be trying to enter his
home, his nerves constantly on edge.

hypervigilance played out. He found himself constantly feeling watched or observed, even when alone,

seek help. What frightened him most was the thought that he might never escape this internal prison,
that he would forever be the "inadequate" person he believed himself to be, perpetually bracing for
a danger that might not even be there.
