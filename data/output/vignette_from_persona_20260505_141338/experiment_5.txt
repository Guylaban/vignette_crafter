role in life – to help, to protect, to make a difference.

him; his heart would race, his palms would sweat, and his breath would quicken, even when he knew

reacting as if he was still in danger even when he was safe.

uniformed personnel. He found himself walking with his head down, purposefully avoiding eye contact
