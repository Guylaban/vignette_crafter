Sanjay felt a profound injustice that the people responsible for that terror had faced no
consequences, no accountability for the damage they had inflicted.

living trapped in a memory, perpetually waiting for the next catastrophe. He desperately wanted to
shed the misery that had become his constant companion, to find a way to quiet the alarm bells that
rang endlessly within him.
