confident in her quick reflexes, yet now a gnawing self-doubt plagued her. She couldn’t shake the
feeling that she had failed to protect them, that perhaps she could have done more, reacted

nothing had truly been done to right the wrong. Her own body, which had once felt strong and

knot tighten in her stomach. The thought of driving with people, of being responsible for another
person’s safety on the road, felt utterly unbearable. She concocted an excuse about needing to stop
for supplies, a small lie that allowed her to drive alone, but left her feeling isolated.

feeling utterly exhausted, hoping to find a way to silence the constant whispers of self-doubt and
reclaim some semblance of the future she had once so eagerly anticipated.
