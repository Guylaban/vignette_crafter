sudden blare of a car horn would jolt him, the sound indistinguishable from the chaos of the
collision.

had even registered a threat, a sudden jolt or tightening in his chest preceding any conscious
thought about what might be wrong.

often walk several extra blocks just to avoid it.

profound weariness, coupled with a desperate hope for a return to his former self, that finally
brought him to therapy.
