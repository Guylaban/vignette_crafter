physically unharmed, witnessed the immediate horror, the frantic scramble, and the agonizing wait
for rescue. The sheer unexpectedness of the disaster fundamentally altered his perception, leaving
him with the profound conviction that the world is a dangerous place, a belief that had only
solidified with time.

In the aftermath, Javier’s internal world shifted dramatically. He soon found his body felt
constantly tense and ready to react to danger, a persistent, low-grade hum of vigilance that never
truly receded. This underlying state meant he was frequently feeling jumpy or easily startled by
everyday occurrences – a door slamming in the office, a sudden car horn on his commute, even his
wife, Elena, moving unexpectedly in the kitchen. These reactions, alien to his former self, led him
to the painful conclusion that his reactions since the event showed that he was a lousy coper, a
stark contrast to the capable professional he had always been.

for his first appointment, his body on alert, hoping for some relief from the relentless fear that
had become his shadow.
