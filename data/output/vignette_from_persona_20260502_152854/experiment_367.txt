his deep conviction that he "Failed to Protect" the other driver. The missing pieces tormented him,
reinforcing his "Punishment Thinking" that he deserved this suffering and confusion.

The "Auditory Recall" was particularly visceral; the screech of tires or the crunch of metal was so
intense that any passing car accident on the news or even a sudden brake sound while he was driving

"Downward Comparison," reminding himself it could have been worse, but it offered little solace.

"Humiliation" and reinforced his "Shattered Beliefs" about his own competence and safety. The
relentless effort to "Suppress Thoughts" about the accident meant he never truly processed the
event, leaving his "Failed to Protect" appraisal unchallenged and growing in intensity. This

struggled to conceal. He’d become withdrawn from his wife, often lost in thought, and his efficiency

memories and stop feeling like a permanently broken man, but mostly, he just wanted the relentless
self-punishment to stop.
