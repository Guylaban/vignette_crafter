back to the suffocating confines of the hospital. These somatic sensations, alongside the faint but

or even from the city air, would immediately plunge her into a deep sense of dread. These triggers,
once innocuous, now activated a profound shift in her perception: she had come to believe that she

danger.

needed to be constantly on guard, Priya began to drastically alter her life. She started avoiding

world growing smaller and more constrained with each passing month. It was the recent experience of
