disorienting cacophony of muffled voices and distant sirens that echoed through his makeshift

Kwame had come to therapy because his carefully constructed strategies were beginning to crumble.

physical intimacy was isolating him, both professionally and personally. He felt trapped, haunted by
a past he couldn’t fully articulate, and terrified that his life as he knew it was slowly slipping
