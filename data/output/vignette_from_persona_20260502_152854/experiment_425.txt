hospital, and the sight of her tiny body tethered to life support machines remained terrifyingly
potent. Though Lily had made a full recovery, and his wife, Mei, had passed away peacefully years
later from unrelated causes, Kian’s world had irrevocably shifted.

Now, Kian’s recollections of that harrowing time were far from a clear, linear narrative; he often
confessed he could not tell a coherent story of what happened even to himself. Instead, the memories

his daily life. Sometimes, he’d hear a child cough or see a medical drama on TV, and suddenly, the
hospital room would feel like it was happening right now, overwhelming his senses with the terror of
that night. These intrusive, present-tense memories drove Kian to constantly keep his mind occupied,

trying to distract himself from the internal chaos.
