serve, unable to protect anyone or anything.

Back in the quiet normalcy of his university campus, the echoes of that experience began to distort
his reality. The sight of a campus security officer’s uniform, or even a police cruiser driving by,

triggers, particularly the visual cue of uniforms and the presence of authority, would immediately
activate a cascade of avoidance behaviors. If he spotted a police car, Kai would instinctively cross

questions or simply remind him of a past he could no longer inhabit.

can't stop bad things from happening to me." This internal battle left him exhausted and on edge.

isolation that finally compelled him to seek help, desperate for some respite from the war still
raging within him.
