feeling of being a victim, would often remind her of the profound helplessness she experienced,
leading to an immediate intensification of her threat response. She had come to believe that the
world was an inherently dangerous place, and that she was constantly at risk. This belief fueled her
need to constantly scan for danger, ensuring the cycle continued unabated.
