identity loss was coupled with a bleak conviction: he believed he had permanently changed for the
worse, an irreparable shift in his core being. The joy he once found in his family and his passion

sanctuary, Kai felt perpetually on guard, unable to truly relax. His wife noticed him scanning
rooms, his eyes darting to windows, his posture rigid even during quiet evenings. This pervasive

his wife at their favorite restaurant, Kai found himself instinctively identifying escape routes

constantly bracing.

constant vigilance, haunted by the belief that his former self was lost forever, and terrified that
he would spend the rest of his life unable to experience the genuine joy and connection he once
knew. He hoped therapy might offer a path back to himself, or at least a way to live with the
stranger he had become.
