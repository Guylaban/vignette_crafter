Even now, years later, Kai's body remained perpetually braced for danger; he described his system as

pervasive physical alertness meant that any sudden movements by others, whether a hurried passerby
in a street market or a colleague reaching for a lens, would immediately put him on guard,
triggering an instinctive tightening in his chest and a surge of adrenaline. Because his body felt

gradually developed a series of strategies to manage this internal storm. He found himself
meticulously planning an escape route before he could even settle into a new restaurant or a
client’s office, his eyes scanning for exits and potential hiding spots. This constant internal
mapping, a desperate attempt to regain control, became a ritual. Furthermore, this ever-present

he was okay, that the world wasn't about to turn on him again.

reinforce it. His constant hypervigilance towards people, for instance, meant he was perpetually

remained primed for threat. When taking on new photography assignments, Kai, who once thrived on
