disconnected snapshots.

profound sense of **identity loss**. She had come to believe that her **life had been destroyed** by
the trauma, a conviction that settled deep within her, making it impossible to envision any future

imminent, and found herself unable to relax even within the familiar walls of her small shop,
constantly braced for the next blow.

bringing back the scene of the accident with such vividness that she momentarily felt she was back
