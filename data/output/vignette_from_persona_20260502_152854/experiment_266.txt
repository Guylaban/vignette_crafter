insurmountable challenge for decades.

any sign of impending danger. He had come to believe, with an unshakeable certainty, that the world
is a dangerous place, a conviction that solidified with each intrusive image and every perceived
threat. This pervasive sense of threat only served to reinforce the memories, making them feel more

eyes darting, his body tense, interpreting casual gestures as potential threats. This incessant need
to be on guard, coupled with the recurring visual memories of his escape, made it incredibly
difficult for him to fall asleep; he felt he needed to stay alert even in his own bed, convinced
that lowering his guard would invite disaster. These persistent feelings of threat, in turn, deeply
entrenched his belief that the world was an inherently dangerous place, and that he was no longer

befallen him and his community, a search for meaning that offered little solace.

prevented him from forming new, safer memories or challenging his deeply held negative appraisals.

push away positive emotions, believing they felt unsafe and would only make him vulnerable to future
pain.

capable of doing the things that once defined him. The avoidance of vehicles, while reducing

experience safe transit, thus maintaining his conviction that the world was still a perilous place.

the way it had stolen his present, leaving him isolated and perpetually on edge, wondering if he
would ever truly feel safe again.
