horrific motor vehicle collision. She had been a passenger in a friend’s car when another vehicle,

mind. She remembered the sheer terror as she braced for impact, convinced she would die. Though she
survived with only minor physical injuries, the world around her had fundamentally changed.

were like a film that played over and over, vivid and intrusive, often making her feel like the
frightening event was happening right now. Remembering the event brought back the same visceral
terror she felt at the time, a sensation that would leave her breathless and trembling. This
constant, vivid replay of the collision made her feel incredibly vulnerable, and she quickly
developed a deep conviction that the world was a dangerous place where you never knew what could
happen next. She had come to believe that people were not what they seemed, and she had to be
especially careful at all times. This overarching sense that the world was inherently hostile meant

potential threat. But her efforts to cope, such as constantly checking her surroundings and
