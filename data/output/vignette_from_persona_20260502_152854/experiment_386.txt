forming any coherent narrative that might bring closure. Simultaneously, **sudden pieces** of the
accident – a flash of red tail-lights, the imagined sound of screeching tires, the cold weight of

**triggering** overwhelming **negative emotions** of horror, searing guilt, and an anger so profound
it felt like a physical ache.

making it **hard to recall deliberately**.

this, watching his life unravel. It was this realization, the terrifying prospect of losing
everything he had left, that finally prompted David to seek help, hoping to find a way to navigate
the labyrinth of his grief and reclaim his life from the shadow of loss.
