fragility of life and the cruelty of others. This profound mistrust has seeped into every aspect of

inability to move past the trauma. This deeply ingrained belief that she is a bad coper, combined

withdrawn at work, struggling to delegate tasks or collaborate effectively with colleagues, always
questioning their motives. This has made her demanding job as a school principal immensely
challenging, draining her of energy and the sense of purpose she once derived from it. The
overwhelming feeling that nothing in her life feels meaningful anymore has cast a pall over her
professional achievements and personal life.
