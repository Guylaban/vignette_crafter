public space, became potent triggers. Each time these triggers appeared, he would feel an immediate
surge of anxiety. This often led him to retreat, spending long hours on his phone, playing games, or
browsing the internet to escape thinking about what he had seen or smelled. This screen escape,

longer capable of doing the things that defined him, especially as his job as a tax inspector
required him to be sharp and focused.

himself anymore, a terrifying thought that made him perpetually on edge. This conviction that he was
