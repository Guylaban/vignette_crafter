everything they thought they understood about safety, authority, and their own body. They had been

lasted forever. Arjun was not arrested. There was no follow-up. But something essential had been
shattered in those seconds: the belief that compliance meant safety, that being calm and cooperative
would protect them, that the world operated according to rules that made sense.

life without their fully noticing it happening. At work, in the open-plan office where they had
