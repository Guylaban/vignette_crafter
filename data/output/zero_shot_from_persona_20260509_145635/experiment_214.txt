# The Weight of Familiar Hands

Boon-Nam is a 72-year-old Thai man who has been married to his current wife for nearly eighteen
years. To the families whose children he tends to as a nanny, he is a gentle and patient presence —

it.

For eleven years, Boon-Nam was in a relationship with a man he had believed would be his partner for
life. The abuse began gradually, the way he now understands these things often do. A shove after a
disagreement. A grip on his wrist that left marks. Then, over time, something more systematic —

though it took a long time to reach that. He stayed because he was frightened, because he was
isolated from his family in Thailand, because the man had convinced him that no one would believe

outside, like one of recovery and stability. He loves his wife. He finds genuine meaning in his work
