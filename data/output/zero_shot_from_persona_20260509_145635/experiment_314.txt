# The Weight of What Was Done to Him

Marcus is eighteen years old, recently hired as a junior tax consultant at a small accounting firm
in the city — a position he earned through a competitive internship program that his mother still
talks about with visible pride. He is the youngest person in his office by nearly a decade, and
until eight months ago, he carried that distinction like armor.

police. The thought of doing so makes him feel, as he puts it, "like I'm already being tried for
something."

produces clean spreadsheets, and rarely makes errors. What his supervisor does not see is that

thighs, his mind replaying images he cannot turn off. He has learned to time these episodes, to

Sleep has become something Marcus negotiates with rather than experiences. He lies awake for hours,
