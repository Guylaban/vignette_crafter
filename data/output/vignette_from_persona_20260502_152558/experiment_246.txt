in the narrative. He can’t remember how he got out of the market or how he got home. This fractured
recall has become a source of profound self-doubt; he feels he can’t trust his own mind, and by
extension, can’t trust that he will do the right thing in a moment of crisis.

perpetually on guard, feeling unsafe even in his own home, has reinforced a devastating sense of

them.
