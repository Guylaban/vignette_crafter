direct line back to the trauma; a fellow dancer’s sudden turn was no longer just a misstep but a
body lurching towards her in the dark, and in that instant, the memory would crash back in, feeling

moving bodies, was simply too dangerous a place to be.

the feeling of a stranger’s footsteps behind her became the terrifying certainty that she was being
followed or tracked, a feeling that sent her scurrying to her apartment, locking the door behind
her. These triggers demanded an immediate, desperate response. She began to avoid going out

holding onto the faint hope that she might one day feel safe enough in her own body to dance again.
