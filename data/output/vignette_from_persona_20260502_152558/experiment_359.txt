the building groaned around him, the intense heat scorching the skin on his arms before he finally
broke through a jammed door.

fragmented sensory snapshots that ambush him without warning. The memory feels like it is happening
right now, not as something that is over. When it comes, he can taste the chalky plaster dust in the
back of his throat, a vividness that often brings on a wave of nausea. This sudden re-experiencing
of the event is a potent trigger, immediately launching him into a state of high alert. The memory

survival. In these moments, he feels an overwhelming need to scan his surroundings for threats and
plot his way out, his mind racing to identify every possible exit from his own design studio.

This pervasive sense of threat has become the baseline of his existence. He finds it impossible to

map he constantly rehearses. This hypervigilance shatters his ability to focus; he describes losing
the thread of conversations and being unable to hold complex design patterns in his mind. The state
of high alert seems to prime his mind for intrusion, and it is often when he is scanning a room for
danger that a fragmented memory of the fire will break through his fractured concentration. The
feeling of being unsafe leads him to seek control where he can find it. At night, this manifests as
a compulsive need to check the locks on his apartment door and windows, sometimes returning to them
five or six times before he can even attempt to sleep.

skin—his immediate response is to flee into activity. He throws himself into his work, staying late,
cutting patterns with a frantic energy that has nothing to do with creativity and everything to do
with distraction. He has come to believe that if he simply stays busy enough, the feelings and
memories cannot catch him. But the exhaustion this creates only makes the intrusions worse when they
inevitably break through. The act of constantly checking locks, meant to bring a sense of security,
instead reinforces his profound conviction that the world is a place of imminent, unpredictable
danger.
