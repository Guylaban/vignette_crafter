blankness. She knew there were significant gaps, entire hours she simply could not account for, and
this lack of a complete narrative left her feeling adrift and untethered from her own experience.

exquisitely sensitive to the few details that do break through. She finds herself scanning every

the sound of heavy, laboured breathing, a noise that seems to echo from the blackness of her memory
