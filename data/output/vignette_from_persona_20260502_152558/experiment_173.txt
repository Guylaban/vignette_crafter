forced his way in as she was locking up for the night. He was smoking, and the acrid smell of his

assault itself.

photographic moments were complete blanks, merciful and terrifying voids where she couldn't recall
what happened at all. This fragmented recollection became a source of constant distress. The images

the very things that brought the memories back in the first place.
