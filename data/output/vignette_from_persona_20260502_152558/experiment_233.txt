wounds have since reshaped his world.

In the weeks that followed the mugging, Arun tried to dismiss the incident, telling Priya he was

nightmares of the attack, waking with a gasp in the deep quiet of his bedroom. The terror from these
dreams did not recede with the morning light; instead, it would seep into his waking hours, leaving
a residue of vulnerability. After a particularly bad night, he would lie awake for hours, listening

cue would instantly heighten his vigilance, making him feel exposed and unsafe even in his own
