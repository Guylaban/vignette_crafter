manifesting as a knot of tension in his stomach and a sleeplessness that leaves him exhausted and

when walking home from a tutoring session, the sound of any motorcycle, no matter how distant, sends

scanning the street for a threat that isn't there. This heightened state of alert means he is
constantly misinterpreting benign cues as signs of danger, turning his daily commute into a series
of false alarms.

people." This core belief that he is unable to do the right thing or accurately read others has, in
turn, made him exquisitely sensitive to potential threats, constantly scanning faces and listening
to tones of voice, which only makes him more likely to encounter triggers.

through his mind as a form of quiet punishment. This belief that he is untrustworthy and that others

it as a dangerous lapse in vigilance. He spends his evenings ostensibly watching television but is
