rage, but it is a rage without an object, hot and useless. He often feels as if he’s watching a film

easy a target. This conviction of his own flawed character haunts the edges of every memory, making
the snapshots feel not just tragic, but deserved.

This sense of personal responsibility now dictates his life. At work, any form of correction has

to escape it, he abruptly stood up, mumbled an excuse about needing coffee, and fled to the
