the house, the weight of a life that had simply stopped—was the locus of her distress.

into her mind, or the feeling of Linh’s limp body in her arms. These fragments are not just
recollections; they are visceral re-experiencings that bring with them a tidal wave of horror and
suffocating guilt. When these images surface, she is suddenly back in that room, her heart pounding
as if the terrible discovery were happening all over again. The intense fear makes her feel as
though she is constantly in danger, and she has not had a full night’s sleep since the incident,
waking often with a jolt of panic.
