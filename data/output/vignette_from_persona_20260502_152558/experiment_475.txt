In the weeks that followed, these fragments began to intrude on his days. The memory wasn't a story

prayer and suddenly see the cracked pavement inches from his face, or be sitting in his quiet
apartment and be ambushed by the scent of old smoke, so real it made him gag. With these sensory
intrusions came a hot, helpless rage that felt alien and shameful to him. This fury was almost
always followed by a cascade of self-recrimination. He had come to believe the event happened
