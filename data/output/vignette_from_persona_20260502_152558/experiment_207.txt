the physical pain and terror that followed.

feels as immediate as the original threat. The smell of burnt food from a neighbor's barbecue or
toast left too long in the toaster can send a jolt of alarm through him, making his heart pound.
This visceral reaction is most disruptive at work. Last month, while on a job, a homeowner began to

moment, the client’s garage felt as dangerous as his old apartment.

assault. To combat this, he has developed a rigid habit of avoidance; he now works late into the
night organizing his truck or doing paperwork, anything to occupy his mind and stave off the
