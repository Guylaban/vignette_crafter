emotionally dead inside has become her new normal. Her work, once a source of pride, is now a
minefield. During a recent planning session, a colleague closed the heavy conference room door, and

but on escape routes; she felt intensely on guard, a false alarm blaring in her nervous system that
she was in mortal danger.

intruder. Her constant state of tension has settled into a permanent knot in her shoulders, ensuring

confined to a life of misery.

dwelling on what she must have done wrong to be targeted. This rumination is a toxic feedback loop;

felt happy, safe, and free.
