and what he did have did not feel like a memory from the past. It came at him as if it were
happening again.

rest stubbornly blank. He would lie awake trying to reconstruct the missing minutes, certain that if

memory broke apart further under pressure, returning as flashes that felt immediate and unfinished.

the liquor store, and the scene arrived with the force of the present tense. If something in the
