warm, then cold, then torrential. He remembers the sound most clearly—not of rain, but of a freight

were separated, or how he eventually hauled himself onto a high ledge, bruised and shivering, but he
remembers the feeling of being utterly, terrifyingly alone.

In the months since, the world has become a minefield. His job, which requires him to drive to

missed appointments and flagging numbers. The natural world, once his refuge, is now a source of
