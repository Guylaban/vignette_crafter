compromised his airway further. The boy survived, but with permanent damage.

tinged mouth. Other times, a high-pitched child’s cry in the grocery store brings another image: the
frantic, jagged line on the portable ECG. These fragments appear suddenly and then vanish, but they
leave her breathless, her heart pounding with the same cold, sickening terror she felt in the back

longer be trusted to save a life.
