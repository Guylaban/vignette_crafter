complete blank, a chasm of lost time until the piercing wail of the ambulance siren filled the
space. It is this blankness that haunts him most. In the quiet of the night, he is certain that he
failed her in those missing moments, that his panic rendered him useless. When he tries to force the
memory, a wave of guilt so profound washes over him that he feels physically ill, and he has to
stop, his heart hammering against his ribs as if trying to escape.
