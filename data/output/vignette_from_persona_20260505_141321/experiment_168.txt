against her cheek, the overwhelming scent of dried oregano, the specific angle of afternoon light
cutting through a high, dusty window. She cannot seem to put the core of the event into words, but
when a fragment surfaces, her body remembers with perfect, terrifying clarity. She will be standing
at her prep station at work and suddenly feel her spine curve and her arms instinctively cross over

This fragmentation of memory has bled into her perception of the world. She has come to believe,

movement of a server rushing past her can cause her to flinch so violently she drops her knife. She
feels threatened by the new delivery driver who brings the produce each morning, watching his every
