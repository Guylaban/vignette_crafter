he waited, certain he would be forgotten there forever.

quickly or the stress of a looming project deadline—is no longer a simple physiological response.

mind, reacting to perceived danger before he has even had time to consciously register a threat.

those with good intentions, are ultimately unreliable and capable of causing harm. This conviction
has made him feel like a permanent outsider, observing the easy camaraderie of his colleagues or the
casual affection between other couples from behind an invisible barrier.

flinch with an alarm that is startling to everyone involved. He has learned to position himself in

experiences as a necessary act of self-preservation. He has also gradually withdrawn from social
