definition quality that makes the rest of his life seem faded and unreal.

kitchen, was enough to make him feel sick and trapped. The most difficult trigger, however, was the
most unexpected. The man who attacked him was later arrested in connection with several other
assaults, and the story was all over the local news. Now, the mere sight of a news van or the
opening theme of the evening broadcast tightens his chest, as if the report is not about a past
event but a future threat.

Keanu began to change his life in small, then significant, ways. He threw himself into his work,
arriving at job sites before dawn and staying until the last light faded, hoping physical exhaustion
would silence his mind. It was a strategy of perpetual motion; he was convinced that if he just kept
busy, the memories couldn't catch him. At home, he became a tyrant in his own kitchen. In a life

weighed his portions, stuck to a rigid diet, and felt a flicker of calm in the strict denial of
appetite. The physical demands of his job, once a source of pride, became another source of terror.

trust between them.

times, the memories bring not fear but an overwhelming grief. He’ll recall a specific moment—the
glint of the streetlights on the motorcycle's chrome fender—and be swamped by a sorrow so profound
it almost knocks him off his feet, a mourning for the man he used to be, the one who felt safe in

He finally sought help after an incident at work. A motorcycle backfired on the street below, and

walls he built to keep the trauma out have become a prison, isolating him from his wife, his work,
and himself. He is terrified that he is permanently broken, but a small part of him hopes he can
learn to live inside his own skin again without being afraid.
