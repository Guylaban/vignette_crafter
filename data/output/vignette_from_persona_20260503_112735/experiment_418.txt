replays relentlessly in her mind, sometimes without any warning, like when she hears a particular
sound or sees something that instantly brings it all back. When Samantha is reminded of the event by
others, her memories become particularly intrusive and overwhelming. This replaying of the traumatic
moment leads her to suppress thoughts about the trauma fiercely, hoping to keep these painful images
at bay. However, suppressing these thoughts only serves to intensify them further; they return with
greater force and frequency. Samantha’s negative appraisals play a critical role in exacerbating her
condition. She has come to believe that there is something fundamentally wrong with her as a person,

painful each time they surface. Her inability to rely on others further complicates matters.
Samantha has started avoiding new friendships or relationships because she fears she cannot trust
anyone else with her pain. She avoids conversations about her trauma, which makes it harder for her

memory replay and triggered memories become more intense and frequent as Samantha tries to avoid
