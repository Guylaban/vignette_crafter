increasingly entangled in a web of avoidance behaviors that stem from his traumatic past. His story

have shaped his current life. John’s most distressing triggers are commands and instructions, which
remind him of the authority figures who issued orders during his time as a refugee. These triggers
are so potent that even today, when John is given an instruction at work or by his wife, he
immediately feels a wave of dread and anxiety. The fear and panic are often compounded by the visual
memories that flood his mind—snapshots of moments from his past where authority figures were issuing
commands that led to dire consequences. The trauma-related events he experienced in Nigeria have
left him with vivid picture-like memories, each representing a moment frozen in time. These images
flash through his mind, sometimes when he is out in public, but often even in the quiet comfort of
his home. The emotionless recall of these moments exacerbates John’s distress; he can see and
remember every detail as if it were happening to someone else, which further isolates him from both
his past and present experiences. John has developed several maladaptive strategies to cope with
these overwhelming memories and triggers. He avoids going out in public as much as possible,
believing that being outside increases the likelihood of encountering situations or people who might
remind him of his traumatic past. This avoidance extends even to social interactions; he actively
avoids other people, finding solace only in isolation where he can control his environment. To
further manage these intrusive memories and triggers, John keeps himself mentally occupied all the
time—working long hours on software projects, engaging in complex puzzles, or listening to music.

not only from his memories but also from his present reality. This dissociative feeling further
reinforces his avoidance behaviors and heightens his sensitivity to triggers. When he does venture
out or engage with others, the slightest hint of an authority command can send him spiraling back
into those emotionally numb moments, reinforcing his belief that avoiding such situations is safer.

memories and triggers so that he can reclaim some sense of normalcy and connection with both his

isolating himself, allowing him to live more fully again.
