the event coherently. Since the accident, Samantha has experienced moments where the memory of that
day seems to return unbidden, and she feels as though she is reliving the crash all over again.

to heighten Samantha’s distress. Samantha's perception of herself has also shifted dramatically. She

appraisals have led to heightened vigilance; she now wakes up multiple times each night to check

Her fragmented memories of the event exacerbate this hyperarousal, as they prevent her from fully
processing what happened. The inability to make sense of her experience has led to further
fragmentation in her daily life. At work, she finds it increasingly difficult to focus on her

campus with heavy traffic and refuses to go on field trips for her classes. Her husband’s attempts

distressing aspect is that Samantha feels as though everything around her could suddenly become

experiences and rediscover meaning in her daily life.
