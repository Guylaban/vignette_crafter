deaths and accidents. He struggles especially when people talk about losing loved ones, as it brings
back the raw pain of his brother’s passing. Marcos had come to believe that these triggers were a
constant reminder of his own loss and failure to protect those close to him. Marcos' coping
mechanisms quickly became maladaptive as he found himself hiding behind his hands during moments
when grief overwhelmed him. He also started picking arguments or creating drama at home to distract
from the trauma. This behavior was often prompted by reminders of his brother, such as photos or
mementos that Marcos now avoided entirely. His wife noticed these changes and grew concerned about
how they affected their relationship. The loss had deeply eroded Marcos’ sense of happiness. He felt
perpetually miserable and believed that he used to be a much happier person before the tragedy. The
weight of his perceived failure to protect his brother contributed to feelings of hopelessness, as
if there was no future where things could get better. This negative appraisal often led him to
engage in more maladaptive behaviors like face hiding and seeking conflict. As Marcos’ sense of
hopelessness intensified, he found himself increasingly drawn into these harmful coping strategies.
He would cover his face with his hands during moments when thoughts of his brother's death
threatened to overwhelm him, only to find that these actions exacerbated his feelings of isolation
and failure. The cycle seemed inescapable: each trigger reminded him not just of the loss but also

his role as a prison guard and husband. These behaviors also triggered further feelings of
detachment and unreality, making it harder for him to engage meaningfully with others. The
cumulative effect of these maladaptive strategies and negative appraisals led Marcos into a spiral
where every trigger felt like a new blow. He found himself isolated from his support system, both at

and failure had left him feeling hopeless about any possibility of recovery. What finally brought

semblance of happiness in life. The weight of his past trauma had become too much to bear alone, and
Marcos understood that professional intervention might be the only path forward.
