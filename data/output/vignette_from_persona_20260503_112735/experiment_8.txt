come to believe there was something wrong with him as a person—a deep-seated belief that he was

situations where he might confront these feelings head-on. He would avoid hospitals, ambulances, and

cycle tightened as Sang’s negative appraisals fueled his maladaptive strategies. Every time he
encountered a trigger—a siren in the distance, a news report about illness—he would retreat further
