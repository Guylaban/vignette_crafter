the aftermath of physical abuse he endured at the hands of a caregiver in childhood. The scars from
those traumatic experiences are deeply embedded in his psyche and manifest in various ways that have
significantly impacted his daily functioning and relationships. The burnt smell that would often

strong physical reactions. When he encounters this scent, which occasionally wafts through the
precinct or from neighbors’ homes during cooking, it plunges him into a state of intense
hypervigilance. He is superalert and on guard, constantly scanning his surroundings for any signs of
threat. These triggers also exacerbate his difficulty in falling asleep as he remains alert
throughout the night. John has come to believe that his reactions since the event show that he is a
lousy coper, which fuels his sense of shattered beliefs about how the world works. He feels that the
abuse happened because of who he is at his core—a belief that intensifies his feelings of guilt and
inadequacy. These negative appraisals have made it nearly impossible for him to explain what
happened clearly; whenever he tries to recount his experiences, he loses the thread and cannot
finish. The intensity of these memories causes him to detach from them, but this detachment only
serves to reinforce his sense that he is a bad coper. When John remembers the event, he feels

just painful; they lead him to avoid wearing clothes similar to what he wore during the traumatic
events. He also avoids other people, feeling that they might remind him of his abuser or the
victims. This avoidance is a maladaptive strategy that has worsened over time, leading John to
withdraw from social interactions and even professional collaborations. The more John detaches

the trauma. This avoidance exacerbates his negative appraisals about his character and coping
abilities, creating a cycle where he feels increasingly isolated. John’s hypervigilance has had
concrete consequences in his work as a detective. His constant need to stay alert and watchful makes

had. Despite this recognition, he is most afraid of revisiting those traumatic memories, yet he
hopes for a way to find some semblance of peace and stability in his life again. Through his

mechanisms, creating a self-perpetuating cycle that has left him feeling shattered and isolated. He
is now seeking help with the hope of breaking free from these negative patterns and regaining
control over his life.
