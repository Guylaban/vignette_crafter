pain in the same parts of his body where he was hurt during captivity. Raj has also developed a

unexpected movements nearby. These startle reactions are frequent and often cause him to freeze or

Raj to adopt maladaptive strategies as a means of coping. He strictly controls what and how much he

afraid of being trapped by these recurring thoughts and feelings but also hopes for a way to break
free from this cycle and regain control over his life.
