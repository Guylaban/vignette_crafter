and pinned. Nothing about the aftermath felt fair; the man was never found, and Leilani was left
with multiple fractures and a deep sense that the world had wronged her.

apartment was secure. Leilani believed that staying alert was the only way to avoid another
catastrophe—especially at night, when she struggled to fall asleep because she needed to remain
vigilant. Her body, she had concluded, could not be trusted to protect her, so she had to do it
herself.
