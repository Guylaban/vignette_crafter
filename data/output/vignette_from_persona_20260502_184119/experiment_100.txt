charges, he tried to convince himself it was over, but the memory refused to fade; it remained more
vivid than ordinary recollections, so extreme that sometimes he caught himself doubting it had
really happened.

threatened he felt, the more often the memory intruded, and the more vivid and real it became. At
the same time, the memory itself acted as a trigger: a single involuntary flashback could send him
