Now at seventy, Meera works as a photographer specialising in portraits and events. The work keeps

trying to block the memory that was already flooding back—the shame, the heat, the same terror she

pulls away.

felt, and the more the memories returned with that same shattering shame and terror, unmoored from

shaking. In that moment, she realised that at seventy, she was still governed by a child’s terror.

carry this shame to the end without ever understanding why she had to carry it at all—but she is
also, quietly, hoping that perhaps she can finally let herself remember what happened without being
destroyed by it.
