gone outside to check on him. The self-blame grew into something solid, and soon she began to
believe that people in general could not be trusted—if she could not rely on herself to protect the
person she loved, how could she rely on anyone else? Even Jason felt different now; he was still
with her, but the quiet between them was heavy with things neither of them said, and Maria had come
to feel that she could not lean on him, because he was already broken by what happened.
