go.

emotions that surface unpredictably. In the middle of a routine sales pitch, a customer’s sharp tone
can trigger a flash of anger that he has to stifle. At other times, guilt washes over him without
warning—he thinks of the people who did not make it home, and he tells himself he should have done
more to help. These feelings arrive and depart on their own schedule, and he has learned to just
breathe through them until they pass.

that darkness itself is dangerous, that the safety of daylight evaporates once the sun goes down. He
avoids evening social plans whenever possible, and if he has to drive after dark, he takes the
longest route to avoid quiet residential streets.

performance issue he hopes to fix with more coffee and longer hours. He does not see a thread
running through them.

to feel that the world is not still an active war zone—to sit through a quiet evening without
scanning the windows, and to sleep through the night in the same bed as the woman he loves.
