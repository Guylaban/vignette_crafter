certain that danger was everywhere, hidden in ordinary places—a closed door, a crowded room, a car
idling too long. The world, she now believed, was a fundamentally dangerous place, and she had to be
on guard all the time because you never knew what could happen next.

moment she walked into any building, mapping routes in her head before she could focus on the people
