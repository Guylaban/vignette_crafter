In the weeks that followed, the world rearranged itself. Karim came to believe that people are not
what they seem, that danger hides behind uniforms and polite routines. He was certain the world is a
dangerous place, and that he had acted in ways that go against who he is—that he had been weak,
complicit. These appraisals became the lens through which he saw every interaction. He began to feel
