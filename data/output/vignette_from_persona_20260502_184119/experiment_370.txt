smell that filled his nostrils, the way his body had betrayed him overnight. He recovered

walking a minefield.

knocked over his chair. The physical reactivity—the racing pulse, the dizziness—only reinforced that
his body was unreliable. Nothing in his life felt meaningful anymore; he was just going through the
motions.

Liam developed strategies to cope, but they looped back on him. He avoided anything discussed in
therapy—if a client brought up medical trauma, he’d steer the conversation elsewhere, or cut the

knew the nightmares would come. When his girlfriend reached for him in the dark, he’d startle, and

at a cost: the avoidance kept his belief that his body was a threat firmly in place.
