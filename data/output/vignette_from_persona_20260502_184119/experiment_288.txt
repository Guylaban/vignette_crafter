protocol could contain chaos, but the hurricane had shown him otherwise: the universe was
indifferent, order was an illusion, and nothing he did mattered. This conviction seeped into
everything. He stopped feeling that his work as a medic had purpose; his wife’s death three years

meaningless. He no longer knew who he was.

refused to believe it. This constant watchfulness, the way his neck muscles stayed tight even when

place was truly secure. His shattered beliefs hardened into a fixed certainty: safety was a myth.
