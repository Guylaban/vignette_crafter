immediate response was to reach for alcohol. She would pour a glass of wine as soon as she got home,

another attack so she wouldn’t be caught off guard. She also began avoiding people entirely,
canceling plans with friends and skipping church.

herself looking behind her constantly, checking if someone was following, even in the grocery store.

made a casual comment about “looking over your shoulder,” and Margaret broke down sobbing. That
night she drank more than usual and could not sleep. Her husband urged her to see a doctor, and she
