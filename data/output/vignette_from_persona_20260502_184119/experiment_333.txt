the gentle brush of his wife’s hand at night could make him flinch, and he would dissociate,

even less able to trust himself, reinforcing his belief that he couldn’t rely on his own reactions.

Over the past year, Amir’s avoidance strategies have taken a toll. He avoids thinking about the
assault during the day, but at night he lies rigidly in bed, listening for sounds that aren’t there,
