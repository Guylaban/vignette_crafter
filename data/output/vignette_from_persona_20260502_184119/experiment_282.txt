the moment. The unwanted thoughts arrived without warning—his own voice saying “I’m sorry” over and
over—and he found himself scanning every parking lot for a figure that might be the offender. A
whiff of alcohol, even from a stranger’s breath at a gas station, sent a jolt through him, his chest
tightening as though the warehouse walls were closing in again.

thoughts did surface, he would grit his teeth and try hard not to think about the trauma, pushing
the images away with a forced focus on his next appointment. More quietly, he noticed a growing
