Margaret is a 65-year-old widow who served for 22 years as a military medic, retiring at the rank of

eleven minutes until the paramedics arrived. He never regained consciousness. He died at the
hospital two hours later from internal bleeding.

In the months that followed, Margaret tried to return to her routines. She kept volunteering at the
VA, but she found herself flinching at every siren. The sound of a car horn would make her drop
whatever she was holding. She stopped driving on rainy days. Then she stopped driving altogether.
She sold Robert’s truck and now relies on her daughter to take her to appointments. She tells me she
