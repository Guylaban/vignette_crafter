**Case Vignette: "Marcus"**

Marcus is a 28-year-old man who presents as neatly dressed, with a composed demeanor that only
occasionally cracks when he discusses his past. He has been in a relationship with his partner,

The story Marcus tells begins five years ago, when he was a specialist in an Army unit deployed to a

drone of the engine, the weight of his gear. Then, the world compressed into a single, deafening

screaming. He helped pull his friend, a gunner named Daniels, from the burning wreckage. Daniels was
alive, but his legs were gone below the knee. Marcus held a tourniquet on what was left, his hands
slick with blood, while they waited for the medevac. Daniels looked at him, not with pain, but with
a terrible, knowing calm, and said, "Tell my mom I love her."

Marcus was not physically injured, but he was medevaced out with the others three days later for
"observation." He was given a few days of rest, then returned to duty. He never spoke of it again

Returning home was a slow unraveling. At first, he was just "on edge." He found civilian life too
quiet, too slow. He missed the clarity of purpose. He took a job in digital marketing, a field he
