**Case Vignette: "Matthew"**

Matthew is a 32-year-old park ranger who has been single since his early twenties. He presents as

For the next 18 hours, they held him in a shallow cave. They bound his hands and feet with paracord.

end of a hot camp stove. They forced him to drink muddy water. They told him, repeatedly, that no
one would find him, that the forest would swallow him. He remembers the smell of pine duff and

He was found the next afternoon by a search team. He was hypothermic, dehydrated, and had three

bill of physical health within two months. But the psychological aftermath has been relentless.

In the present, Matthew still works as a ranger, but he no longer takes solo patrols. He volunteered
for a desk shift twice a week, handling permits and visitor questions. This feels like a demotion,
but he cannot bring himself to go into the backcountry alone. When he drives a patrol truck through
dense forest, his heart rate spikes. He checks his rearview mirror constantly. He has to pull over
and breathe when the road narrows between two rock outcroppings, because the shape of the cave wall
comes back to him.

details. When someone at the station jokes about the "bad campers" they’ve dealt with, Matthew’s
stomach turns. He excuses himself. He has started taking the long way to the bathroom to avoid

four times a week. In the dreams, he is always in the cave, but the men are faceless, and his radio
is dead. He wakes up gasping, his hands clenched into fists. He has not been able to sit with his
back to a door in a restaurant or a coffee shop since the attack.
