**Case Vignette: Dr. M.**

Dr. M. is a 63-year-old married North American physician who presents for an initial consultation at
the urging of his wife. He is a tall, fit man with a measured, deliberate way of speaking, though

concussion.

For the first year after returning home, Dr. M. did not speak about the attack. He told his wife he
was "fine," that he was "processing it in his own way." He returned to work within three weeks. But
his wife noticed changes. He stopped watching the evening news. He became irritable during family
dinners, snapping at his adult children for small things. He began drinking two glasses of whiskey

In the past year, the symptoms have worsened. Dr. M. now avoids the hospital break room because it

his life caring for others, and now, for the first time, he is allowing himself to be seen as
