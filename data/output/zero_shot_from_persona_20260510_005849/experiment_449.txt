**Patient Vignette: "Mara" (Age 27, Non-binary, Oceania)**

Mara arrives to sessions with a quiet, deliberate stillness, as though they are constantly listening
for something just out of earshot. They are a special education teacher at a public elementary

new relationship with Alex, and were feeling hopeful for the first time in years. That same month, a

laminating visual schedules, when they heard the tap of a key in the lock. The door swung open. Mr.
Vance stood there, holding a thermos of tea. “I thought you might be cold,” he said. He had a master
key. He had a copy of the building’s after-hours access code. Mara froze. They thanked him, took the
tea, and did not drink it. They did not report it. They told themselves he was just a lonely, over-
involved parent. They told Alex it was “nothing.”
