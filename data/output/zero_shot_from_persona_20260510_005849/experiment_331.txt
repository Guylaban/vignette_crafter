The event that fractured her life occurred eighteen months ago, on a Tuesday afternoon in late

filled with the smell of gas, dust, and something metallic. She could hear her own heartbeat in her
ears, a frantic, trapped bird.

paintings—thirty years of work—were ruined by smoke, water, and debris. The canvas she had been
working on, the one with the ochre and green, was sliced open by a shard of glass. It looked, she

In the months afterward, Evelyn attempted to return to her work. She bought new supplies, set up a
