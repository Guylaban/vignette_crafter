sound of his belt being unbuckled. The beatings were not for disobedience, Tom says, but for "being

hypervigilance, his ability to see the lie before it was spoken, made him a brilliant detective. He
has a near-perfect case closure rate.

But the detective’s tools are now his prison. In session, Tom describes the past six months as "a
