**Case Vignette: “The Ledger”**

The patient is a 75-year-old woman of Oceania heritage, married for 52 years, and a retired auditor

She was rescued by a neighbor who pulled her from the rubble. She lost the hearing in her left ear

meticulous auditor, and kept a house so orderly that every spice jar faced the same direction. She
thought she had left the war behind.

But her body did not forget. For decades, she has been unable to stand in a crowd at a bus stop

waiting for the sky to tear open again.
