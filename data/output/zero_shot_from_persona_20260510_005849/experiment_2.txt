**Patient Vignette: Mr. R.**

Mr. R. is a 67-year-old Latin American man, a widower of three years, who presents for therapy at

decades, known for his relentless pursuit of justice in cases of violent crime. He arrives to his

tried to bury the event under a mountain of case law. He succeeded, for a time.
