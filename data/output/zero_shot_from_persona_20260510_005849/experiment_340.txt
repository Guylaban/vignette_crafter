**Vignette: "The Weight of the Siren"**

The patient is a 33-year-old married woman, a paramedic with ten years of service, who presents for

The event occurred on a rainy Tuesday in late October. She was the lead paramedic on a code response

involving young men, and last month, she froze at the wheel of the rig during a non-emergency

of the antiseptic smell of the ER. She has intrusive, vivid images of the boy’s mother’s face—a
