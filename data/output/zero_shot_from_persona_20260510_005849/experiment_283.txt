**Patient Vignette: Maria S.**

Maria is a 38-year-old single woman who came to therapy at the urging of her sister. She presents as
a factory worker on an assembly line for a large automotive parts manufacturer, a job she has held

The story Maria tells begins on a Tuesday evening, two years ago. She had just finished her shift at

eight, a quiet man who worked as a night janitor at a local school. That night, she called him from
her car to say she was stopping for groceries. He said he was tired, that he had a headache, and
that he would see her when she got home. She remembers the flatness in his voice, but she dismissed
it.

phone. The note said he was sorry, that he couldn’t handle the loneliness anymore, that he felt like
a ghost in his own life. Maria performed CPR until the paramedics arrived, but he was already gone.

In the months that followed, Maria did not cry. She went back to work three days after the funeral.
She told herself that the factory was where she needed to be, that the noise and the repetition were

She stopped sleeping. At night, she would lie awake, her heart pounding, her skin prickling with a
