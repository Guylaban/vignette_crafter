**Case Vignette: Kofi**

Kofi is a 25-year-old journalist from Ghana, currently living and working in a major U.S. city. He

journalist, but the words wouldn’t come out. He remembers the cold metal of handcuffs, the dark

He was let go after twelve hours, with no charges and no apology. His editor retrieved him. He went
home, showered, and tried to sleep. He told Amara it was nothing, just a misunderstanding. For the
first few weeks, he threw himself back into work. He told himself he was fine. He told himself it
was part of the job.

Now, six months later, Kofi is not fine. He has stopped taking assignments that involve any kind of
crowd. He cannot walk past a police car without his heart beginning to pound. The sound of a siren
makes his hands shake, and he has started taking the long way to work to avoid the precinct near his
