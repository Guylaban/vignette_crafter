**Case Vignette: Dr. K.**

Dr. K. is a 77-year-old retired surgeon, a slender man with steady hands that still move with

The event that haunts him occurred in April 1975, when he was 27 years old. He was a junior surgical

man was still alive, pinned under a beam, his face white with shock. Dr. K. tried to lift the beam,
but he could not. He held Dr. Minh’s hand as the man whispered, “Tell my wife I was trying to save
them.” Dr. Minh died before help came. Dr. K. left the hospital that night, walking through the
rubble, and did not look back. He escaped the country three days later.

In the present, Dr. K. does not speak of the war. He built a successful career in the United States,
becoming a respected general surgeon. He prides himself on his calm demeanor in the operating room.
