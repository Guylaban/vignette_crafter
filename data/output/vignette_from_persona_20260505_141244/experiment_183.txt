wash over her, an impression that would haunt her long after the sirens faded.

found herself avoiding driving altogether, convinced that the very act of getting behind the wheel
would lead to another catastrophic event. The mere thought of being a passenger turned her stomach;
she could picture the screeching tires and the impending impact at any moment. This avoidance
extended beyond the road; she began to shun any situation that would put her body under stress,
whether it was exercising at the gym or even engaging in a spirited conversation with friends.

in, filling her with the conviction that to relax was to invite danger.

surface of everyday life. She had come to see her body as unreliable, a traitor that had allowed her

had she done to deserve such a fate? The world seemed markedly unfair, and Clara struggled to
reconcile her life’s purpose with the chaos that had befallen her.

missed invitation solidified her isolation, and she often found herself spiraling into thoughts of

life she once led was reduced to a series of anxious days, filled with scanning for exits and
avoiding places that reminded her of her trauma.

could begin to feel safe again.
