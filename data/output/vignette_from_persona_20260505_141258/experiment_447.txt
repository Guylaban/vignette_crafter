In the weeks that followed, the memory did not settle into the past the way she had expected it to.

courthouse where she practices — could stop her mid-sentence. A door slamming somewhere down the

briefly but completely, to the sound of her own breathing against that pillar. She found herself

she had to press her fingernails into her palm to remain in the room.
