truck out to survey sites before dawn meant long stretches in the dark — stretches that had become
almost unbearable. Waking in the night in her rural home, with no ambient light and the particular

unexpected direction — any of these could send her pulse into her throat. She had come to believe,

without feeling like she was walking into danger.
