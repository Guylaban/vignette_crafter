eventually evacuated in a convoy, Aleksander watched through the vehicle window as parts of the town

unharmed.

for. There was fear that arrived without warning — sharp, physical, and disproportionate to whatever
was in front of him at the time. There was horror that seemed to attach itself to ordinary moments.

shame he found harder still to articulate: a sense that he had been helpless, that he had not done
enough for his family, that something about him had been permanently exposed. These feelings did not
diminish with time. If anything, they settled more deeply into him.
