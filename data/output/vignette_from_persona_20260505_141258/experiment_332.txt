the small back room of the house, which her aunt would sometimes lock from the outside so that

most terrifying experiences. She learned to associate the natural world not with freedom but with
exposure and inescapability.

Adaeze emigrated in her early twenties and rarely spoke of her childhood. For many years the

tree-lined path to reach the entrance. On overcast days, when the light falls in a particular way

physical alertness she cannot explain or dismiss — her muscles tighten, her breathing shallows, and

as her body preparing for something she cannot name.
